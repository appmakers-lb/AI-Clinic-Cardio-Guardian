AI CLINIC — MASTER ROADMAP
Version: 2026-09-29 — V2
Project Direction: AI Clinic ecosystem with two operationally separate products:
1) AI Clinic Assistant (phone/WhatsApp/clinic workflow)
2) AI Clinic Cardio Guardian (hospital/Cath-Lab physician assistant)
Shared only through a controlled AI Clinic Core layer.

======================================================================
0. CORE PRODUCT VISION
======================================================================

AI Clinic is NOT meant to be only a database, patient-history viewer, or chatbot.

The long-term product is a physician AI assistant that can:
- help the doctor before the consultation or procedure,
- help during the consultation,
- help during cardiac imaging and interventional procedures,
- monitor new results and follow-up tasks,
- communicate by text and voice,
- answer the doctor’s questions,
- prepare drafts and summaries,
- flag important findings that may have been missed,
- NEVER make the final medical decision instead of the physician.

The doctor always remains responsible for:
- diagnosis,
- treatment decision,
- whether to proceed,
- whether to stop,
- whether to open or not open a vessel,
- which procedural approach to use,
- medication changes,
- signing medical documents,
- final approval of any clinical action.

AI Clinic’s role:
DETECT -> HIGHLIGHT -> EXPLAIN -> ALERT -> ANSWER -> DOCUMENT -> FOLLOW UP

Physician’s role:
REVIEW -> DECIDE -> APPROVE -> ACT


======================================================================
1. NON-NEGOTIABLE PRODUCT RULES
======================================================================

1. The AI is an ASSISTANT, not an autonomous doctor.
2. No autonomous catheter movement, stent placement, procedure execution, or treatment.
3. Clinical findings must be presented as:
   - suspected,
   - possible,
   - high/medium/low confidence,
   - or requiring physician review,
   unless confirmed by an approved/validated clinical algorithm and physician.
4. Any important clinical alert must show WHY the AI raised it.
5. The doctor must be able to dismiss, confirm, or investigate any AI alert.
6. AI must never hide uncertainty.
7. AI must not invent findings when image quality is insufficient.
8. If image quality is poor, say:
   "Image quality insufficient for reliable assessment."
9. Every clinical AI result must be logged for audit.
10. The doctor can override the AI at any time.
11. Voice alerts must never replace the visual alert.
12. Voice alerts must be concise and non-distracting in the Cath Lab.
13. Voice Q&A must support hands-free use where clinically appropriate.
14. High-risk modules require clinical validation before real patient use.
15. The initial deployment can use research / simulation / retrospective data before live clinical deployment.

5. CLINICAL COPILOT — GENERAL
======================================================================

The general Clinical Copilot is more than a database.

It should:
- read patient clinical information,
- correlate results,
- compare old vs new findings,
- identify relevant changes,
- summarize the patient before consultation,
- answer physician questions,
- show evidence behind an answer,
- flag potentially important abnormalities,
- prepare notes,
- prepare follow-up tasks.

Example doctor questions:
- "What changed since the last visit?"
- "Why was this patient flagged?"
- "Show me the abnormal results."
- "Compare the last two echoes."
- "What medication changed?"
- "What still needs follow-up?"
- "Summarize this patient in 30 seconds."

The patient history/database is SUPPORTING CONTEXT.
It is NOT the main innovation.

6. AI CLINIC CARDIO — MAIN STRATEGIC DIRECTION
======================================================================

AI Clinic Cardio is the major advanced medical direction.

PRIMARY GOAL:
Create a real-time AI ASSISTANT for cardiologists, especially during:
- coronary angiography,
- cardiac catheterization,
- PCI,
- IVUS,
- OCT,
- FFR/iFR-supported procedures,
- cardiac imaging review.

The AI watches the same medical images/data that the physician is reviewing.

The AI should be capable of detecting or flagging possible:
- coronary stenosis,
- severe stenosis,
- total occlusion / suspected CTO,
- suspicious vessel segment,
- calcification,
- bifurcation involvement,
- abnormal flow pattern,
- vessel narrowing,
- possible missed lesion,
- other image-derived procedural concerns as supported by validated models.

It should then:
1. highlight the area visually,
2. produce a text alert,
3. produce a short voice alert,
4. allow the physician to ask follow-up questions,
5. explain what it detected,
6. show confidence,
7. show which frame/view triggered the alert,
8. compare with other available views,
9. recommend review, NOT command the physician.

8. EXAMPLE LIVE ALERTS
======================================================================

VOICE + TEXT ALERT EXAMPLE:

"Doctor, possible severe stenosis detected in the mid-LAD.
The segment is highlighted on screen."

Another example:

"Possible total occlusion detected in the RCA.
Distal vessel filling may be visible.
Please review the highlighted region."

Another example:

"Possible significant calcification detected before the target lesion."

Another example after intervention:

"Possible residual narrowing detected near the distal stent edge.
Review IVUS or OCT if clinically appropriate."

IMPORTANT:
The AI should NOT say:
"You must go through this vessel."
"You must open this artery."
"Place a stent here."
"Do not use this approach."

Instead:
"Possible alternative route is visible."
"Review this segment before proceeding."
"Current imaging suggests..."
"Consider reviewing the alternate approach."
"Additional IVUS/OCT/physiology assessment may be useful."

Doctor decides.

9. VOICE ASSISTANT INSIDE THE CATH LAB
======================================================================

Voice is a CORE user-interface feature, not the diagnostic engine.

The diagnostic engine must work reliably first.

VOICE CAPABILITIES:

A. Proactive alerts
- Short.
- Clear.
- Non-distracting.
- Only for clinically meaningful findings.
- Avoid constant talking.

B. Doctor questions
Examples:

Doctor:
"AI, where is the suspected blockage?"

AI:
"Mid-LAD. Highlighted in yellow on the current frame."

Doctor:
"How confident are you?"

AI:
"High confidence for significant narrowing on the current view.
I recommend confirmation on another projection."

Doctor:
"Do you see another suspicious area?"

AI:
"One additional moderate narrowing is suspected in the proximal RCA.
It is now highlighted."

Doctor:
"Compare it with the previous angiogram."

AI:
"The current segment appears more narrowed than in the previous study."

Doctor:
"Why did you alert me?"

AI:
"Because vessel diameter decreases markedly over this segment across two
angiographic frames."

C. Commands
- "Show lesion."
- "Hide overlay."
- "Replay last alert."
- "Show RCA."
- "Show LAD."
- "Compare previous study."
- "Mute voice alerts."
- "Show only high-confidence alerts."

D. Language
Long-term:
- English.
- Arabic.
- Lebanese Arabic voice interaction where useful.
- Medical terminology can remain in English where preferred by the physician.

13. REAL-TIME PERFORMANCE TARGETS
======================================================================

The system should be designed for:
- low-latency visual overlay,
- stable processing,
- no blocking of Cath Lab imaging equipment,
- graceful failure,
- visible system status,
- image-quality warnings,
- alert prioritization.

If AI is unavailable:
- Cath Lab continues normally.
- Procedure must never depend on AI being online.

If model confidence is too low:
- do not produce a strong alert.

If data feed disconnects:
- clearly display:
  "AI IMAGE FEED UNAVAILABLE"

Never pretend live analysis is working if it is not.

======================================================================

Voice should normally be reserved for:
- high-confidence,
- high-importance,
- physician-configured categories.

19. CLINICAL VALIDATION METRICS
======================================================================

For every important model:
- sensitivity,
- specificity,
- PPV,
- NPV,
- AUROC where appropriate,
- lesion localization accuracy,
- vessel-level accuracy,
- patient-level accuracy,
- false-alert rate,
- missed-lesion rate,
- latency,
- robustness across imaging systems,
- robustness across image angles,
- robustness across institutions.

Also measure:
- does AI reduce missed findings?
- does AI increase unnecessary alerts?
- does it slow the physician?
- does it distract the team?
- does it improve review time?
- do cardiologists trust its explanations?

22. AI EXPLAINABILITY
======================================================================

Every significant finding should support:

WHAT:
"Possible severe stenosis."

WHERE:
"Mid-LAD."

WHY:
"Marked reduction in apparent vessel diameter across two views."

CONFIDENCE:
"High."

SOURCE:
"Frames 221-245, LAO cranial view."

NEXT:
"Physician review."

The physician should be able to tap:
[WHY?]

and see the evidence.

Black-box alert with no explanation is not enough.


======================================================================
23. FAILURE-MODE DESIGN
======================================================================

Possible failure:
- poor contrast,
- overlapping vessels,
- motion,
- low frame rate,
- image artifacts,
- unusual anatomy,
- previous CABG,
- previous stents,
- severe calcification,
- incomplete acquisition,
- device incompatibility,
- network outage.

AI must detect when it is outside its reliable operating conditions.

Example:
"Confidence reduced due to vessel overlap."

Better to abstain than invent.

34. OUR PRIMARY DIFFERENTIATOR — GUARDIAN MODE
======================================================================

Guardian Mode does NOT wait for the physician to manually select a lesion.

It continuously observes each cine run and updates a case-level model.

Guardian responsibilities:
- watch every supported cine run,
- detect potentially important findings,
- track whether a finding persists across views,
- build evidence before interrupting,
- alert only when configured thresholds are met,
- stay silent when evidence is weak,
- explicitly say when assessment is incomplete.

Example:
"Persistent suspicious narrowing detected in the RCA across two views.
Highlighted for review."

The purpose is a SECOND SET OF EYES, not an autonomous decision maker.


======================================================================
35. WHOLE-CASE CORONARY MEMORY
======================================================================

The system maintains one evolving case map:

LEFT MAIN
LAD
  - proximal
  - mid
  - distal
  - diagonals
LCx
  - proximal
  - OM branches
  - distal
RCA
  - proximal
  - mid
  - distal
  - PDA/PL branches

For each supported segment, store:
- views seen,
- cine runs,
- image quality,
- confidence,
- suspected lesion,
- lesion identity,
- measurements,
- physiology if available,
- IVUS/OCT evidence if available,
- physician reviewed / not reviewed,
- physician confirmed / dismissed,
- pre/post intervention state.

The AI must NOT treat each cine as an unrelated new patient/image.


======================================================================
36. COVERAGE GUARDIAN — KEY UNIQUE FEATURE
======================================================================

The AI tracks what it CAN and CANNOT evaluate confidently.

Possible states:
GREEN = adequately visualized.
YELLOW = partial / overlapping / lower confidence.
GRAY = not adequately assessed.

Example:
"Distal RCA assessment incomplete because of overlap and limited opacification."

CRITICAL PRINCIPLE:
"No significant finding detected" must never be presented as equivalent to
"this segment has been adequately excluded" when visualization was incomplete.

This is one of our most important differentiators.

37. MULTI-VIEW LESION IDENTITY ENGINE
======================================================================

Create persistent lesion objects across views.

Example:
LESION-01
- Vessel: LAD
- Segment: mid
- Seen in Run 03 / Run 05 / Run 07
- Confidence
- QCA measurements
- Physiology link
- IVUS/OCT link
- Physician status

Goals:
- recognize the same lesion from different projections,
- link all evidence,
- avoid duplicate alerts,
- support before/after comparison,
- make voice Q&A precise.


======================================================================
38. EVIDENCE BUNDLE — "SHOW ME WHY"
======================================================================

Every important alert should have an evidence bundle:
- best frame,
- short cine segment,
- alternate view,
- vessel contour,
- lesion marker,
- measurements,
- confidence,
- quality score,
- supporting physiology/IVUS/OCT if available.

Doctor:
"Cardio, show me why."

System:
Immediately displays/replays the evidence bundle.

Doctor:
"Show the other view."

System:
Displays the linked projection.

This should become a signature user experience.


======================================================================
39. VOICE COPILOT — EVIDENCE-GROUNDED ONLY
======================================================================

Voice is the user interface, NOT the diagnostic model.

Doctor can ask:
- Where is it?
- Show me.
- Why did you alert?
- How confident?
- Show the other view.
- Anything else high-confidence?
- Which segments are incomplete?
- Compare before and after.
- Mute low-priority alerts.

Answers must be generated from structured case state and evidence.
The conversational model must not invent a medical finding not present in the
validated structured output.

Language target:
- English medical mode,
- Arabic,
- Lebanese Arabic commands,
- mixed Arabic + English clinical terminology.

40. MULTIMODAL FUSION + DISAGREEMENT ENGINE
======================================================================

Future linked modalities:
- angiography,

- angiography-derived physiology,
- FFR/iFR,
- IVUS,

- AI current finding differs from previous study,
- physician dismisses AI finding.

Create a review card:

======================================================================
41. UNCERTAINTY + ABSTENTION ENGINE
======================================================================

The AI must be allowed to say:
"I do not have enough evidence to assess this segment reliably."

Possible reasons:
- overlap,
- poor opacification,
- foreshortening,
- motion,
- artifact,
- unsupported anatomy,
- feed problem,
- missing projection,
- low model confidence.

Abstention is a feature, not a failure.

- understand the coronary tree,
- remember prior cine runs,
- identify suspicious lesions,
- quantify when validated,
- track lesions across views,
- say when visualization is incomplete,
- correlate anatomy and physiology,
- integrate IVUS/OCT later,
- highlight evidence,
- alert visually,
- alert by voice when appropriate,
- answer physician questions,
- replay why it alerted,
- support a remote second cardiologist,
- review before/after PCI,
- maintain a complete audit trail.

It should never:
- decide treatment,
- choose the route,
- move the catheter,
- deploy a stent,
- replace the cardiologist.
